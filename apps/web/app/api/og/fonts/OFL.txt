They are committed rather than fetched at runtime because Satori falls back
over the network for unknown glyphs, and that fallback fails silently in
sandboxed builds — producing tofu in the one artifact this product depends on
reaching a family intact.

TTF rather than WOFF2: Satori cannot decompress brotli.
