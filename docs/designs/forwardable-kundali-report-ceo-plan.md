## Vision

### 10x Check

The report as designed is a one-shot artifact for one pair: two charts in, a verdict out,
everything forgotten. That is how every kundali tool works, which is why they are all
commodities.

**A birth chart is not a question. It is a key.**

Once PREMA holds a chart, the engine that compares two people compares that person against
everyone who has joined. That is not a calculator; it is a market. The cost is low because
`calculateGunaMilan` against N stored profiles is the same function in a loop.

**Scope honesty on this vision:** it describes Approach B, not this push. This plan
captures only an anonymous signal that people *want* it (see Accepted Scope 2). Any
pool arithmetic below is illustrative, not projected. Per-user guna floors, not a
universal one — design-doc premise 2 stands as revised and is not reopened here.

### Platonic Ideal

You are 27, in Pune. For eight months your mother has sent you biodata PDFs. Every one
ends the same way: the chart comes back wrong, and you both pretend that does not sting.

You open PREMA once. Date, time, Pune. Thirty seconds.

What comes back is quiet. A handful of people whose charts clear yours, each with a line
reading **32/36 · Nadi clear · no Bhakoot dosha**, set in the visual language your mother
already trusts, because it looks like what the pandit hands her.

You press one button. She reads numbers she recognises, and for the first time in eight
months does not have to ask anyone whether it is alright.

Then she calls you. Not to object. To ask which one you liked.

**The moment is not the match. It is the argument that does not happen.**

A ₹2,400 crore incumbent growing under 1% while a challenger a third its size compounds
at 21% is a saturated model. Share is moving. This is a stronger market argument than
anything in the design doc, which argues from founder conviction alone.

**Warning:** human-assisted matchmaking is already monetised by incumbents, and families
already pay a pandit per consultation. The paid tier below therefore tests whether PREMA
can be a **channel** for a good families already buy. It does **not** test pool demand.

## Premise Challenge (CEO altitude)

The design doc names **liquidity** as the binding constraint and measures **share rate on
a free report**. Different hypotheses; the plan could not tell them apart.

Inversion: failure is not a low share rate. Failure is a 35% share rate, delighted users,
a viral free calculator, and nobody who wants to be in a pool.

**What this plan does and does not fix.** The paid upgrade is real demand evidence for a
channel. The anonymous opt-in signal measures stated interest in a pool, cheaply and
honestly. **Neither proves pool demand.** An earlier draft of this plan claimed a consent
checkbox was "not free" and therefore real signal — that was wrong. A checkbox costs one
click and is weaker evidence than the waitlist signup this document mocks. The claim is
withdrawn; the measurement is kept, labelled as interest rather than demand.

## Accepted Scope

Baseline is design doc Approach A. On top of it:

1. **Paid co-signed upgrade.** One button under the free result offering an astrologer's
   review. Hosted payment link. Manual fulfilment. **Price: name it before building** —
   anchor against what a family pandit charges per consultation, since that is the
   substitute good. An unnamed price makes the result uninterpretable.
   **SLA: 3 business days. Refund: no questions asked.** Both printed on the page before
   the button ships.
   **Ethics clearance (binding, from the committed human-factors rule):** the upgrade
   renders **identically at 32/36 and at 11/36**. No copy implying payment can change or
   rescue a verdict. No countdown, no scarcity, no urgency. This is the product's first
   payment surface and it sits directly beneath the most emotionally loaded moment PREMA
   creates. Targeting the upsell at disappointed users is exactly what
   `.agents/skills/prema-human-factors/SKILL.md` forbids.

2. **Anonymous opt-in signal.** Render the checkbox; record `{timestamp, opted_in, city}`
   and nothing else. **No personal data, therefore no deletion path, no retention
   statement, no DPDP surface.** The opt-in *rate* is the metric. Build the real capture
   only if it clears the threshold in Success Criteria.

3. **Trust bundle.** Eight koota explanations plus Nadi and Bhakoot dosha explainers,
   written from scratch, in a register a parent trusts. Permanent disclosure of the Lahiri
   ayanamsa and gender-neutral convention. **Translations are web-page only in v1; the PNG
   stays English plus Devanagari koota names** — Tamil is a separate script needing a
   second embedded font, and the design doc already names the font embed as the most likely
   silent failure.

4. **Ask-the-pandit handoff.** A `wa.me` message containing both people's name, DOB, birth
   time and city as **plain text the astrologer casts from themselves**. It cannot contain
   a lagna chart — the engine computes Moon longitude only. Keep the payload well under
   ~2,000 characters. **Ships together with the divergence disclosure or not at all**;
   routing users to a verdict PREMA does not control is only survivable when the page has
   already explained why the numbers may differ.

5. **Report persistence.** Store the report payload behind an opaque id at generation
   time. Payment carries that id. Column A is "you"; **column B is never persisted in any
   form, including logs.** The two-person form makes consent scope ambiguous unless this
   is stated in the plan rather than left to a ticket.

### From the 11-section review (all four accepted)

6. **Order ledger.** Payment webhook writes every paid order to Supabase with status. Work
   from the table, not your inbox. A three-day promise with no ledger is a promise you
   cannot keep on purpose.
7. **Instrumentation.** Eight events plus a one-page dashboard: `report_generated`,
   `share_clicked`, `optin_checked`, `pandit_clicked`, `pay_clicked`, `pay_completed`,
   `geocode_miss` (with city), `birthtime_missing`. Your primary metric currently has no
   means of collection.
8. **Zero silent failures.** Require birth time or render a visible caveat band on both the
   page and the PNG; a clear message plus coordinate fallback when a city is not found; a
   build-time check that the Devanagari font is embedded. Silently guessing noon and
   presenting a confident 36-point score is the one failure this product cannot survive.
9. **Pay button behind an env flag** so money can be switched off without a deploy.

## Success Criteria (revised — thresholds, samples, decision rules)

| Metric | Numerator / Denominator | Threshold | Min n | What each outcome decides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Share rate** | `share_clicked` / `report_generated` | ≥20% | 100 reports | Below: the forwarding thesis is dead. |
| **Opt-in rate** | `optin_checked` / `report_generated` | ≥15% | 100 reports | Below: do not build the real capture or the list view. Above: build the capture *with* a contact field, then re-measure. |
| **Paid conversion** | `pay_completed` / `report_generated` | ≥2% | 100 reports | Above: PREMA can be a channel for astrologer services. Below: the channel thesis is weak. |
| **Gate** | fixture births matching external reference | 20/20 | — | Blocking. Nothing ships otherwise. |

**Disagreement rule.** High share and near-zero opt-in is the failure mode this plan
predicts: a delightful free calculator nobody wants a pool for. **If that combination
appears, Approach B is dead** and the next session is about the channel business, not
the pool. Write that down now so it cannot be rationalised later.

**First 100 must come from a named channel.** Your own network plus one specific community
— a WhatsApp group, a subreddit, an alumni network. Name it before launch; there is no paid
acquisition, so an unnamed channel means the experiment cannot conclude.

## Corrections applied after outside-voice review

1. **`explanation-engine.ts` cannot be reused.** An earlier draft claimed the trust bundle
   reuses it. It ranks the seven *blended dimensions* (the scorers explicitly out of scope),
   requires a full `FullCompatibilityOutput`, and its entire astrology output is four
   threshold strings on `gunaTotal`. The per-koota strings in `ashtakoota.ts` are debug
   output (`"Vashya compatibility score: 2/2"`). Item 3 re-estimated from ~2h to ~4-6h plus
   translation procurement, and reclassified from wiring to writing.
2. **Table count.** The repo has **16** Supabase tables. This plan adds a 17th (orders) and
   an 18th (events); the anonymous opt-in signal can share the events table. All new tables
   need an anonymous-insert RLS policy — the existing schema is `auth.uid() = id` owner-only,
   which a public web form cannot satisfy.
3. **Workspace wiring is four steps, not one.** `packages/compatibility` is `private`, has
   `"main": "./dist/index.js"`, **no `exports` map**, and a standalone CommonJS `tsconfig`
   that does not extend `@repo/typescript-config`. `dist/` is unbuilt and `turbo.json`
   outputs are `.next/**` only. Required: add `"exports": {".": "./src/index.ts"}` (or build
   first and add `dist/**` to turbo outputs), add `transpilePackages: ["@prema/compatibility"]`
   to `next.config.js` (currently `{}`), add the workspace dependency, and reconcile
   TypeScript versions — root and web pin 7.0.2, compatibility devDeps ^5.0.0, mobile ~6.0.3.
4. **Manglik is cheaper than claimed.** `astronomy-engine@^2.1.19` is already a dependency
   and supplies geocentric Mars longitude directly. "Days of work" was overstated. Cutting
   Manglik from v1 remains correct on scope grounds, but note that both the paid report and
   the pandit handoff route to a human who **will** check it.
5. **Open question 2 resolved: remain gender-neutral.** Consequence: the divergence note is
   permanent and the fixture stays at nakshatra/pada level. If the paid tier ships, extend
   the fixture to 20 *pairs* with per-koota breakdowns — money is attached to the 36-point
   total, and the current gate does not verify it.

## Corrections to the documents above

1. **`calculateVarna` cannot be fixed as specified.** Varna is intrinsically gendered
   (1 point if groom's varna >= bride's, else 0). Correction #5 chose gender-neutral. No
   coherent symmetric rule exists: "equal only" scores ~25% of the time and depresses every
   total against the reference; "either direction" is what the code already does. **The rule
   is undefined, so the mandated regression test cannot be written yet.** Batched to the
   astrologer.
2. **There are more than two Ashtakoota defects.** `calculateVashya` averages an internally
   inconsistent matrix — Vanachara's row is all zeros while every other row gives Vanachara
   1 or 1.5. Chatushpada x Vanachara = (1.5 + 0)/2 = **0.75**. Tara and Graha Maitri also
   yield halves. **Real totals will read "27.75 / 36".** No reference tool prints quarter
   points and no document has a rounding or display rule. `calculateGana`'s averaging is a
   no-op — `getScore` is already symmetric.
3. **Nadi's cancellation rules are hand-rolled and unverified.** 8 of 36 points, the most
   disputed koota, and headlined on the page as a "Nadi dosha verdict."
4. **The gate verifies the moon calculator, not the total.** Nakshatra and pada agreement
   covers the moon position. Six kootas get no external verification at all, and money is
   attached to the 36-point total.
5. **`noUncheckedIndexedAccess: true`** at `packages/typescript-config/base.json:13`.
   Wiring `packages/compatibility` source-only pulls it into `apps/web`'s strict program
   and **`npm run check-types` will fail** — the source indexes unchecked throughout
   (`NAKSHATRAS[Math.min(index, 26)]`, `pairs[0]`, `vashyaMatrix[a]?.[b]`). Unbudgeted.
6. **Every runtime dependency is absent from `package-lock.json`**: `luxon`, `tz-lookup`,
   `@supabase/supabase-js`, `tsx`, `satori`. `node_modules` is not installed.
7. **No CI exists.** No `.github`, no `test` task in `turbo.json` or `package.json`, and
   `vitest.config.ts` includes `__tests__/**/*.test.ts` which does not exist. "Nothing ships
   unless the gate passes" currently has zero enforcement.
8. **Instrumentation must insert server-side with a secret key**, not through an
   anonymous-insert RLS policy. A public unauthenticated write endpoint whose contents are
   your primary metric is trivially forgeable, and client-side events lose a material share
   of Indian traffic to ad-blockers — biasing the *denominator* specifically.
9. **`tz-lookup` is probably unnecessary.** It is a multi-hundred-KB geodata blob that
   returns `Asia/Kolkata` for effectively the whole wedge. Put the IANA zone in the city
   JSON you already have to ship. Keep Luxon.
10. **`ImageResponse` runs on the Node runtime**, so the edge-compatibility probe is mostly
    moot. Choosing Node explicitly removes the bundle-ceiling question.
11. **Vercel Hobby forbids commercial use.** The moment the pay button is live the project
    must be Pro.
12. **Payment KYC needs live Terms, Privacy, Refund and Contact pages on a registered custom
    domain** — not `*.vercel.app`. Those pages are INC1 content. **The long pole's
    prerequisite sits in the increment shipping first**, and nothing listed it.

## Caveats on the slicing

**All three metrics share one denominator** (`report_generated`, min n=100). Shipping INC1
to real users burns it: opt-in rate and paid conversion then have no cohort. The increments
can be **built** separately; whether they are **released** separately requires deciding
whether the n=100 clock restarts. It has to.

**The statistics are undecidable at n=100.** Paid conversion >=2% crosses on two purchases,
95% CI roughly [0.2%, 7%] — spanning "dead" and "great". Opt-in >=15% carries +/-7 points.
Either raise n or label INC3 a qualitative probe.

**n=100 may be unreachable.** Solo, no paid acquisition, channel still unnamed. A personal
WhatsApp network realistically yields 20-40. Estimate reach before committing three weeks.

## The metric correction that came out of designing a state

Designing the share states surfaced a measurement bug in the plan:

**`share_clicked` is not a forward.** A user can open the share sheet and back out, and
the plan would have counted it. The designed "sent" state fires **`share_completed`**,
reached only when `navigator.share` resolves. An abandoned share sheet is never counted.

This matters because the share rate is the primary success criterion. Instrument
`share_completed`, not `share_clicked`. Keep both if you want the abandonment rate,
but the threshold is measured on completions.

## The ethics rule, now visible in the design

The upgrade surface at **11/36 is byte-identical** to the surface at 32/36 — same
position, same wording, same weight. No "improve your score". No "a review may help".
No countdown, no scarcity. Returning from an abandoned checkout lands on an unchanged
page with **no recovery nudge**: payment abandonment is an answer, not an objection to
overcome.

This was asserted in prose in the CEO plan. It is now a designed state that an
engineer can compare against.

## User journey — the gap left open (Pass 3, 3/10)
