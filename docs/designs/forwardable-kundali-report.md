## Problem Statement

In Indian matchmaking, kundali (birth chart) compatibility is a real gate with real veto
power. The sequence today runs in the worst possible order:

```
family finds a candidate → emotional investment begins →
charts go to an astrologer → VERDICT → if no: start over
```

The astrologer is a gate at the *end*. By the time the chart is checked, two families have
met and hopes are up. The expensive part is not the consultation fee or the three-day wait
— it is investing in a person the chart was always going to reject.

PREMA's thesis: relocate that veto to the front. Only ever surface people whose chart has
already cleared.

## Demand Evidence

**None external.** The founder's answer to Q1 was "I have the conviction myself." No users,
no one asking for it, no one paying. This is the honest starting point and is stated here
so that no downstream skill mistakes conviction for validation.

Everything in this document is a hypothesis. The recommended approach exists specifically
to produce the first real evidence within a week.

## Status Quo

The family astrologer / pandit. Families take two birth charts to an astrologer, pay per
consultation, wait days for a verdict that arrives with no introduction attached.

Adjacent, already-solved commodities: AstroSage, Astroyogi, mPanchang and JyotishApp all
give free 36-point guna milan away. Development guides price a kundli matching app at
USD 5,000–15,000. **The calculation is not defensible.**

Western astrology dating apps (NUiT, Stars Align, Align, CosmoPair, MetYet, StarCrossed,
Boo) exist in quantity and none are large.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**User:** 25–32, wants to choose their own partner *and* wants the chart to clear so their
family blesses it. Not the parent running the search.

**What is actually being purchased: permission, not prediction.** The user does not need to
know their guna score. They need an artifact they can forward to their mother that ends the
argument. This reframe (from the cross-model review) is the single most load-bearing
conclusion in this document, because it determines the deliverable (a document, not a feed),
the surface (WhatsApp, not the app), and the metric (was it forwarded, not did they swipe).

**Wedge:** one web page that turns two birth inputs into a forwardable kundali match report.

## Constraints

- Ethics rule is committed to the repo (`.agents/skills/prema-human-factors`): never exploit
  loneliness, FOMO, romantic insecurity, artificial scarcity or emotional vulnerability to
  drive engagement or payment. **This closes the dominant monetization model in the category**
  (pay-per-minute astrological reassurance). Accepted knowingly.
- Solo builder, pre-revenue, no users.
- No test infrastructure of any kind exists in the repo.

## Premises

1. **The engine is not the moat.** Guna milan is free everywhere; the defensible asset is a
   pool of people who already pass each other's charts. *Agreed.*
2. **Astrology should be a floor, not 50% of a blended score — and the floor belongs to the
   family, not the config file.** *Revised during session. Applies to Approach B only —
   Approach A is a two-person report with no pool and no gate.* Each user declares what their
   family actually checks (some only Nadi dosha, some only Manglik, some nothing); PREMA
   computes, displays prominently and sorts by guna, and hard-gates only on what that user
   asked for. Rationale for the revision: an app-computed 28/36 carries no authority with a
   family who will consult their own pandit anyway, so a universal floor adds an
   unauthorised pre-screen in front of the real veto while PREMA eats the false negatives —
   and a ninth hard filter on top of the eight already in `hard-filters.ts` is unaffordable
   at zero users.
3. **The Zomato/District concierge is a separate company.** It depends on a partnership not
   yet started, and solves nothing until matches are already going on dates. *Agreed.*
4. **The binding constraint is first users, not features.** *Agreed.*

Findings from direct inspection during this session, each verified against the source.

| **Dead config** | `discoveryThreshold: 70` is declared in `matching-config.ts:42` and `types.ts:240` and referenced nowhere in the engine. (`maxRecommendations: 5` **is** used — `ranking.ts:63,80,83,115,116`.) |
| **Astrology gates nothing** | `hard-filters.ts` blocks on blocking, safety, activity, gender, age, distance, intention, children. Guna milan is not among them; it only moves the score. |
| **Weights** | astrology 0.50 (gunaMilan 0.70 / vedicSignals 0.20 / manglik 0.10), relationshipGoals 0.15, personality 0.12, lifestyle 0.10, communication 0.08, interests 0.05, values 0.00, numerology 0.00 (`metadata_only`). |
| **Manglik is a constant** | `manglik.ts` contains no Mars calculation. So `checkManglik` always returns `'unknown'` and `astrology-scorer.ts` hardcodes `manglikScoreRaw = 75`. The 0.10 manglik sub-weight is a constant. |
| **Two Ashtakoota scoring defects** | `calculateBhakoot` (`ashtakoota.ts:120`): `distance = ((B-A+12)%12)+1; reciprocal = 14-distance`. Opposite signs give `distance=7 → reciprocal=7 → pairs=[7,7]`, matching no auspicious pattern → **score 0**; the intended `1/7` branch is unreachable because `distance=1` always yields `reciprocal=13`. `calculateVarna` (`ashtakoota.ts:13`): two `>=` checks award 1.0 on equal ranks and 0.5 otherwise — it can never return 0 and returns a half-point on a 1-point koota. |
| **Gendered-convention deviation** | The engine is deliberately gender-neutral: `calculateVashya` averages both directions, `calculateVarna` is symmetric, `calculateGunaMilan` takes profiles in arbitrary order. Varna, Vashya, Tara, Bhakoot and Nadi are traditionally computed with groom/bride asymmetry — which is what AstroSage does. PREMA's 36-point total will systematically differ from the reference every user checks against. |
| **Schema ahead of product** | 16 Supabase tables including `algorithm_versions`, `compatibility_events`, `post_date_feedback` — versioned-algorithm telemetry for zero users. |

**Pitch/code mismatch:** the founder describes the product as matching by "astrology and
numerology." Numerology is weighted 0.00 and is display-only. Either weight it or stop
describing it as part of the match.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: The Forwardable Report — CHOSEN
Fix the moon calculator, then ship one web page that takes two birth inputs and returns a
36-point Ashtakoota report built to be forwarded to a parent. Effort S (~48h with CC+gstack; ~1 week solo unaided). Risk Medium.

### Approach B: Wire What You Have
Connect Supabase, wire `(tabs)` discovery to the engine, activate the dead config, open a
closed beta. Effort L (human ~6-8 weeks / CC ~1 week). **Rejected as the next step:** builds
liquidity infrastructure before anyone has asked to be in the pool, and ends with the same
zero demand evidence. Correct destination, wrong next move.

### Approach C: The Co-Signed Report
Approach A plus one real astrologer reviewing and signing each report, charged per report.
Effort M. **Deferred, not rejected** — it answers the authority objection an algorithm
structurally cannot, and layers cleanly onto A once the artifact exists.

## Recommended Approach

**A**, because it is the only option that can falsify the central bet inside a week, using
the one substantive asset in the repo, and it costs nothing that B or C would need later.

**Risk: Medium**, not Low. The stack is bleeding edge (Next 16.3.4, React 19.2.8,
TypeScript 7.0.2) and two items below are unbudgeted-complexity risks flagged in review.

### Hour 0–8 — the gate. Nothing else ships until this passes.

1. **Fix the timezone bug, both halves.** Line 52 of `moon-calculator.ts` is a *compound*
   error. The known half is treating IST birth time as UTC (~3.0° drift). The worse half is
   that `getFullYear()/getMonth()/getDate()` read in the **server's** local zone while the
   rest of the expression is `Date.UTC` — on a non-UTC machine a midnight DOB shifts a full
   calendar day, which is 13.2°, an entire nakshatra, four padas.
   **Fix:** read Y/M/D from the DOB as a plain `YYYY-MM-DD` string (matching the `DATE`
   column in Supabase), resolve city → lat/lon → IANA zone via `tz-lookup`, then combine
   date + birth time + zone in Luxon with historical offset.
2. **Fix `calculateBhakoot`.** The `1/7` branch is unreachable. Decide the intended
   convention and make the reachable cases match it.
3. **Fix `calculateVarna`.** Must return 0 or 1, never 0.5.
4. **Write `scripts/chart.ts`** — a ~15-line CLI harness (tsx or `node --experimental-strip-types`)
   that takes birth details and prints the full koota breakdown. Nothing else in the repo can
   currently run the engine; The Assignment below depends on this existing.
5. **Write the repo's first test:** a golden fixture of 20 births verified against an external
   reference. Selection is where the value lives, so it is specified:
   - ≥6 within 0.2° of a pada boundary
   - ≥3 within 0.5° of a nakshatra boundary
   - ≥2 births before 1947
   - ≥1 birth in 1942–45 (India ran a +6:30 wartime offset; IST was adopted in 1906)
   - the remainder spread across Indian time zones and cities
6. **Decide Manglik, explicitly.** `manglik.ts` has nothing to audit — there is no Mars
   calculation and `isManglik` is never written. Computing it properly needs Mars longitude
   *plus* lagna (sidereal time + lat/lon + a house system), which is days of work, not hours.
   **Recommended: cut Manglik from the v1 report and say so on the page.** A report that
   always prints "Manglik: unknown" is worse than one that does not claim to check it.

### Hour 8–36 — the artifact.

1. **Wire the workspace.** `apps/web` is the unmodified Turborepo starter and nothing under
   `apps/` references `@prema/compatibility`. Required: add the workspace dependency; add
   `transpilePackages` to `next.config.js` (currently `{}`) or order the build; add `dist/**`
   to the `build` task outputs in `turbo.json` (currently `[".next/**"]` only, so `dist/` is
   never cached or restored). "Import it directly" is three config changes, not zero.
2. **One page**, two people: name, DOB, birth time, city.
   - **City → lat/lon** needs a bundled India city dataset (a few hundred cities covers the
     wedge) or a named geocoding API with a stated failure mode. `tz-lookup` takes
     coordinates, not place names. This is a hole at step one of the pipeline.
   - **Missing birth time:** the engine currently falls back to 12:00 and downgrades
     confidence to `moderate`. For an artifact meant to be forwarded as authoritative,
     decide: require birth time, or render a visible caveat band on the output. Recommended:
     require it, since the whole product is trustworthiness.
3. **Output:** full 36-point Ashtakoota breakdown, Sanskrit koota names, Nadi and Bhakoot
   dosha verdicts. **Apply `.agents/skills/prema-human-factors/SKILL.md`** — `CLAUDE.md`
   makes it mandatory for any user-facing screen, and this is the user-facing screen. Three
   concrete decisions it forces: minimum body text size for the stated 55-year-old reader,
   which scripts render (English / Hindi / Devanagari for koota names), and whether the page
   ever implies urgency.
4. **PNG at WhatsApp forward dimensions** via `next/og` (Next 16 ships OG generation as
   `next/og`; `@vercel/og` is the older package). **Budget the font embed** — Satori does not
   fetch fonts, so Devanagari koota names require an embedded font ArrayBuffer or they render
   as tofu in the forwarded image. This is the single most likely silent failure.
5. **State the AstroSage divergence on the page.** The engine is gender-neutral; AstroSage
   is not. Either collect bride/groom roles in the form and adopt the gendered convention, or
   print a one-line note explaining why totals may differ. Users *will* cross-check.

### Hour 36–48 — distribution.

- `wa.me/?text=` share as the single primary CTA. The forward is simultaneously the
  distribution mechanism and the is-this-real test.
- **Interest capture without a database:** a second `wa.me` link — "tell me if you want in" —
  costs zero hours and zero liability. **Do not build the phone-capture table.** Storing a
  non-consenting third party's name, DOB, birth time and city as a "cold-start asset" needs
  an anonymous-insert RLS policy against an owner-only schema, adds a 17th table, tests
  nothing about the stated hypothesis, and sits against both the committed ethics rule and
  India's DPDP Act. If any storage happens, store the score and share flag only.

**Explicitly out of scope for these 48 hours:** the Expo app, all twenty gallery mockups, all
sixteen Supabase tables, numerology, the personality/communication/lifestyle/interests/values
scorers, PremaPlus, every concierge screen, the admin app, Manglik, and the Zomato/District
integration.

## Open Questions

**Live for Approach A:**

1. **Does an app-computed verdict carry any family authority at all?** If not, Approach C
   (a real astrologer co-signing) stops being optional. Share rate is a partial proxy.
2. **Bride/groom roles:** does the report form collect them? Deciding yes means adopting the
   gendered koota convention and matching AstroSage. Deciding no means permanent divergence
   from the reference, which must then be stated on the page.
3. **Manglik:** cut from v1 (recommended) or budget Mars + lagna as its own phase.
4. **Numerology:** weight it, or delete it from the pitch. Currently neither — it is
   `metadata_only` at 0.00 while being described as half the matching mechanism.
5. **Lahiri ayanamsa precision:** the convention is not in doubt — `getLahiriAyanamsa()`
   (`moon-calculator.ts:21`) implements Lahiri as the linear approximation
   `23.856 + 0.01396*(year-2000)`, accurate to roughly an arcminute. Confirm it matches Swiss
   Ephemeris to <0.01° across the fixture date range, then close this.

**Deferred to Approach B — do not build until B is chosen:**

6. **Does a user-set guna floor starve the pool?** Settle it by synthesising 2,000 profiles on
   a realistic Indian urban birth distribution, running the engine, and plotting surviving
   candidates at floors of 0, 18, 24, 28 intersected with the eight existing hard filters.
   Hypothesis: at floor 18 the median user loses roughly half their already-filtered pool; at
   24+ the median sees single digits at launch scale. This is a substantial harness for a
   decision that only matters once a pool exists. Approach A has no pool and no gate.

## Success Criteria

- **Primary:** ≥20% of users who generate a report share it. **Minimum sample: 100 reports.**
  A share rate on n=3 is not evidence. The first 100 must come from a named channel — the
  founder's own network and one specific community (a WhatsApp group, a subreddit, a college
  alumni network). Name it before launch; there is no paid acquisition in this phase, so if
  the channel is unnamed the experiment cannot conclude.
- **Gate:** all 20 golden-fixture births agree with the external reference on nakshatra and
  pada, and the Bhakoot and Varna fixes are covered by cases in the fixture. Note this gate
  does **not** prove agreement on the 36-point total — the gender-neutral deviation above
  means totals may legitimately differ. Extending the fixture to 20 *pairs* with per-koota
  breakdowns is the stronger version, and is recommended if the gendered convention is adopted.
- **Secondary:** reports generated per week, and inbound replies to the "want in" link.

## Distribution Plan

Web only. `apps/web` is Next.js — deploy to Vercel, auto-deploy on merge to main. No app
store, no binary, no release pipeline. Distribution *is* WhatsApp forwarding; there is no
paid acquisition in this phase by design, because the share rate is the experiment.

## Dependencies

- `luxon` (timezone-correct instant construction) and `tz-lookup` (lat/lon → IANA zone).
  Neither is currently in the repo. Pin exact versions; "or equivalent" is not acceptable on
  a gate-blocking dependency.
- **An India city → (lat, lon) dataset**, bundled, or a named geocoding API. `tz-lookup`
  cannot take a place name, so without this the stated input ("city") does not reach the
  calculator.
- **A Devanagari font file** embeddable as an ArrayBuffer, if koota names render in
  Devanagari in the PNG.
- An external reference for the golden fixture (AstroSage output or Swiss Ephemeris).
- Workspace wiring: `@prema/compatibility` as a dependency of `apps/web`,
  `transpilePackages` in `next.config.js`, `dist/**` added to turbo build outputs.
- A Vercel project for `apps/web`.
- No third-party partnership required. Zomato/District explicitly deferred.

## The Assignment

**First write `scripts/chart.ts`** (item 4 of the gate — ~15 lines, and nothing in the repo
can currently run the engine without it). **Then take three real birth charts — yours and two
people you know — run them through it, and run the same three through AstroSage. Put the two
sets of numbers side by side.**

You will see the timezone bug with your own eyes rather than taking two AI agents' word for
it, and you will know exactly how wrong the output is today. Then send that comparison to
one person in your life who actually had their kundali matched, and ask them one question:
*"when your family did this, what did they actually do with the result?"*

That answer tells you whether the artifact you are about to build is the artifact they need.
